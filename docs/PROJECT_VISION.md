# AI RAW Editor

**You shoot. AI edits. You approve.**

AI RAW Editor is a photographer-first professional RAW editing desktop application. The photographer captures the images, the software assists with individualized edits, and the photographer reviews and approves the final results.

The initial MVP uses adaptive image processing, not yet a trained AI retoucher. Semantic masks, reference-guided grading and professional retouching are roadmap items, not completed features.
